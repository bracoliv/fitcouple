How to use FitCouple

⚠️ The instruction below is for your AI (ChatGPT / Grok / Gemini / Claude). Paste it together with your sheet link.

1. Copy the sheet template

Open the template → File → Make a copy → save it in your Google Drive.

Open template

2. Share your sheet

Click Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer.

3. Connect with your AI

Use ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Claude, or any AI that can see images. Paste the full instruction below.

4. Copy this full AI instruction
