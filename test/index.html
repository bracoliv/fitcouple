How to use Fit

Solo, Couple, and Family each keep their own Google Sheet. Do not mix them.

1. Pick a dashboard

One person, two people, or a family.

2. Copy the template

Open template, then File → Make a copy.

3. Share it

Anyone with the link → Viewer, then paste that link into the dashboard. Do not post the link online. This site does not store your sheet.
