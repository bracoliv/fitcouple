Refunds

Edit this page before you take orders. It is a starting point, not legal advice.

These are physical goods from the seller named on the Shop tab. If you bought on Shopee, Shopee's refund rules apply and you contact the seller there.

If you paid through the seller's own checkout, contact the seller with the phone number on the Shop tab within 7 days of delivery. Say the order date and whether the goods were damaged, wrong, or unopened. The seller refunds the price you paid. There is no separate app credit, promo code, or points refund.

A sandbox payment is a test. No card is charged, so there is nothing to refund.
