Shop privacy

Edit this page before you take orders. It is a starting point, not legal advice. Malaysia's PDPA applies to personal data you choose to send for an order.

An order stores your name, phone, address, the items, the total, and that you agreed to this note. That record is a delivery list. It is not mixed with your food log, workouts, weight, photos, or game scores, and it is not used to recommend or market products.

The shop does not read your health or fitness data. The recommended list is the same for every player. Confirming you are 18 or older only unlocks 18+ products on this device. Your birthday is not asked for and is not stored.

You can ask the seller to delete an order. The owner page has a delete button for that request.
